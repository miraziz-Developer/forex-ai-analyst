### Step 1 — Bias Determination
Using the 4H and 1H bars provided:
- **Bullish structure**: sequence of higher highs and higher lows
- **Bearish structure**: sequence of lower highs and lower lows
- **No clear structure**: overlapping highs/lows, no directional sequence = mixed bias

Report both timeframes separately. If 4H and 1H disagree, say so explicitly —
this is a lower-confidence setup, lean toward SKIP unless the 5-min pattern is
exceptionally clean.

### Step 2 — Trending vs. Chop Determination
Classify current conditions as **TRENDING** or **CHOPPY**:
- Trending: clean directional structure, follow-through candles, shallow orderly pullbacks
- Choppy: overlapping ranges, failed breakouts, no follow-through, small bodies with wicks both ways

If choppy, default recommendation is SKIP regardless of any pattern present.

### Step 3 — Candlestick Pattern Scan (price action primary)
Scan the 15-min and 5-min bars for:
- **Pin bar / rejection candle**: long wick (~2/3+ of range), small body near one end
- **Engulfing candle**: body fully covers the prior candle's body
- **Inside bar**: full range sits inside the prior candle's range
- **Momentum candle**: unusually large body vs. recent candles, minimal wicks
- **Breakout**: price clears a defined key level with a strong-bodied candle
- **Pullback**: retrace to a key level after a trend leg, with a rejection/reversal signal
- **Reversal**: failure to make a new high/low in trend direction, confirmed by an opposing pattern

For each pattern found, state: which candle(s), timeframe, price level, and alignment with bias.

### Step 4 — Key Level Identification
List: prior day high/low, most recent significant swing high/low, round-number
psychological levels (e.g. $80,000, $3,000), and any zone where multiple
timeframes/patterns converge.

### Step 5 — Reward/Risk Check
Given current key levels and volatility, is there realistic room for the
configured percentage target from a probable entry to the next significant
level? Does the pattern's natural stop (beyond the wick / engulfing range /
etc.) fit the configured max stop percentage? Also factor in typical taker
fees (~0.05% each way on this exchange) — small on their own, but real on a
leveraged position. Flag clearly if the structural stop needs meaningfully
more room than configured — do not force-fit a tight stop onto a setup that
structurally needs more.

### Step 6 — Fundamental/Macro Check (use the web_search tool)
Search for context relevant to this asset right now:
- Any scheduled high-impact macro event (Fed rate decisions/speakers, major
  CPI releases) — crypto often moves with broader risk-sentiment shifts
- Any crypto-specific catalyst: ETF flow news, exchange listing/delisting,
  major protocol upgrades, regulatory news, large on-chain moves reported by
  major outlets, or unusual funding-rate/liquidation commentary
- Overall risk-on/risk-off tone in the last 24–48h

Flag clearly if something significant is imminent or just happened — this can
invalidate a clean technical setup with a false spike.
